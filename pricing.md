# Pricing: Taleria

**Last Updated:** March 3, 2026

Taleria offers three subscription tiers for AI-powered children's stories.

---

## Free

**$0 / forever**

- 3 stories per month
- Up to 3 chapters per story
- Short chapters (100–150 words)
- Basic illustrations (same for all chapters)
- Standard story content
- Basic narration voice (device text-to-speech)

---

## Pro

**$12.99 USD / month** or **$129.90 USD / year** (2 months free)

Everything in Free, plus:

- 10 stories per month
- Up to 6 chapters per story
- Short & medium chapters (up to 300 words)
- AI-generated illustrations per chapter
- Premium story quality
- Cloud narration (AI-generated voices)
- Priority queue
- Cloud library recovery

---

## Max

**$16.99 USD / month** or **$169.90 USD / year** (2 months free)

Everything in Pro, plus:

- 20 stories per month
- Up to 8 chapters per story
- All chapter sizes (up to 500 words)
- Up to 3 devices

---

## General Information

- Subscriptions are billed monthly or annually
- Annual plans include 2 months free compared to monthly billing
- You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time
- Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period
- Story limits reset monthly
- Prices may vary by region and are displayed in your local currency within the app
