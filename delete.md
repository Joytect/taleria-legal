# Data Deletion Request

**Taleria** — AI-Powered Stories for Children

---

## How to Request Data Deletion

To request the deletion of your data associated with the Taleria app, please send an email to:

**[joytect25@gmail.com](mailto:joytect25@gmail.com?subject=Taleria%20Data%20Deletion%20Request)**

In your email, please include:
- The subject line: **"Taleria Data Deletion Request"**
- A brief description confirming you want your data deleted

We will process your request within **30 days** and send you a confirmation once completed.

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request data deletion, the following data stored on our servers will be **permanently removed**:

| Data Type | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Device identifier | Deleted |
| Story usage quotas | Deleted |
| Order history | Deleted |
| Subscription-device mapping | Deleted |

---

## What Data Is Retained

| Data Type | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Subscription purchase records | Managed by RevenueCat and Google Play / Apple. Subject to their respective data retention policies. |
| Cached story content (R2 storage) | Shared content cache, not linked to individual users. |

---

## On-Device Data

All locally stored data (child profiles, saved stories, settings) can be deleted at any time by:
1. Opening the Taleria app
2. Going to **Settings** and deleting individual profiles and stories
3. Or simply **uninstalling the app**, which removes all local data
